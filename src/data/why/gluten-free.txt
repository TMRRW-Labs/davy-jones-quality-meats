EYEBROW: CLEARER INFORMATION
HEADING: Know What You're Buying.
- Two Different Designations | We separate Gluten Free from No Gluten Added so customers can understand the difference at a glance.
- Manufacturer-Labelled Gluten Free | Products marked Gluten Free come to us pre-packaged and carry the manufacturer's gluten-free designation.
- No Gluten Added In-House | Products marked No Gluten Added are prepared in our shops without intentionally added gluten-containing ingredients.
- Cross-Contact Is Possible | Our shops are not gluten-free facilities, and products containing gluten are handled in the same environment.
- Ask Before You Buy | If you have celiac disease, a serious sensitivity or a food allergy, speak with our team and review the product label before purchasing.
- Clear Product Tags | Look for the Gluten Free or No Gluten Added designation on individual products throughout the site.
