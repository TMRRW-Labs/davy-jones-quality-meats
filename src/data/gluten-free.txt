## No Gluten Added | no-gluten-added
> These products are prepared in our shops without intentionally added gluten-containing ingredients. Because our shops also handle products that contain gluten, cross-contact is possible and we describe these items as No Gluten Added, not Gluten Free.
> If you have celiac disease or a serious sensitivity, talk to a butcher and we'll walk you through how the item is made.
