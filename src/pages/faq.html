The Davy Jones Difference

What you get from a butcher shop

I’ve never shopped at a butcher before. Do I need to know what cut I want?

Not at all. Tell us what you’re making, how many people you’re feeding or what you like, and our butchers can help you choose the right cut.

Does custom cutting cost extra?

No. Custom cutting is part of the service. Choose the thickness, trim, portion size or bone-in or boneless preparation you want and we’ll prepare it that way.

Can you help me choose the right cut?

Yes. Tell us what you’re cooking and how you plan to prepare it, and our butchers can recommend a cut that suits the meal.

Can you give me cooking advice?

Yes. Our team can help with cooking methods, temperatures and preparation based on the cut you’re taking home.

What products do you make in your shops?

Our burgers, sausages and Butcher Crafted products are prepared by our own team. Butcher Crafted includes stuffed, seasoned, wrapped and ready-to-cook favourites made in-house.

The Meat

Sourcing, Aging and Specialty Cuts.

Where does your beef come from?

Our beef is sourced from Alberta and selected for the marbling and quality we want at the counter.

Why is your beef aged a minimum of 21 days?

Aging gives the beef time to develop the tenderness, texture and flavour we look for before it is cut and sold.

Why do you choose grain fed beef?

We choose grain fed beef for the marbling it develops, which contributes to tenderness, texture and flavour.

What makes your Premium Dry Aged beef different?

Our dry aging program is managed in house and produced in limited quantities. We focus on well marbled cuts such as prime rib with availability changing depending on what is ready.

Do you carry specialty products beyond beef, pork and chicken?

Yes. We carry a changing selection of specialty meats and seafood, which may include A5 Wagyu, lobster tails, scallops, salmon and shrimp. Availability varies, so call the shop if you are looking for something specific.

Freezer Packs & Larger Orders

Planning ahead

How do freezer packs work?

Choose from one of our standard freezer packs, then customize it with our team if you’d like to make changes. Packs can include a mix of beef, pork, chicken, sausage and other everyday favourites.

Can I customize a freezer pack?

Yes. Freezer packs can be adjusted to better suit your household and packed to your specifications. Changes may affect the final price.

How long does a freezer pack take?

Please allow up to one week depending on the size of the pack and product availability. Pricing may also change with market conditions, and we’ll confirm everything when you order.

Can I place a larger order?

Yes. For larger orders outside of our freezer packs, 1 to 2 days’ notice is recommended so we have time to prepare everything properly.

Gluten & Allergens

What We Can and Cannot Guarantee.

What’s the difference between Gluten Free and No Gluten Added?

Products marked Gluten Free come to us prepackaged and carry the manufacturer’s gluten free designation. Products marked No Gluten Added are prepared in our shops without intentionally added gluten containing ingredients. Because our shops also handle gluten, cross contact is possible.

Can you guarantee No Gluten Added products are safe for someone with celiac disease?

No. We cannot guarantee that No Gluten Added products are free from cross contact. If you have celiac disease or a serious gluten sensitivity, please review the product label and speak with our team before purchasing.

Can I ask about ingredients or how a product is prepared?

Yes. Tell us which product you are considering and our team can explain the ingredients and how it is prepared.
