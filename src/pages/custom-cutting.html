How It Works

Simple, Custom and Usually While You Wait.

01. Tell Us What You Need

Start with the meal, the cut or the result you have in mind. You do not need to know the exact butcher terminology.

02. We Cut It Your Way

Choose the thickness, trim, portion size, or bone in or boneless preparation you want. If you are unsure, our butchers can help you decide.

Most everyday custom cutting can be done while you wait. Larger orders may require advance notice.

What To Ask For

If you're not sure how to ask, ask like this.

“Two-inch ribeyes, four of them.”

Give us the cut, thickness and how many you need.

“A roast that'll feed eight.”

You don't need to know the weight. Tell us how many people you're feeding and we'll help you size it.

“Trimmed for the smoker.”

Tell us how you're cooking it and we'll prepare the cut with that in mind.

You do not need to know the terminology. Just tell us what you are trying to make.

FAQ

Questions About Custom Cutting.

Does custom cutting cost extra?

No. Custom cutting is included. Tell us the thickness, trim, portion size or how you want it prepared.

Do I need to know exactly what cut I want?

No. Tell us what you're making, how you're planning to cook it and how many people you're feeding. We'll help you choose from there.

Can you help me figure out how much I need?

Yes. Give us the headcount and what you're making, and we'll help you work out an appropriate amount.

Do I need to order ahead?

Not for most everyday cutting. For larger orders such as a whole prime rib, freezer pack or party order, call a few days ahead.

Will you tell me how to cook it?

Yes. Ask at the counter. We'll help you choose the right cut for what you're making and walk you through how to cook it.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll get your order ready. For larger orders, give us a few days' notice.

What are your hours?

Point Edward is open Tuesday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM and Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.
