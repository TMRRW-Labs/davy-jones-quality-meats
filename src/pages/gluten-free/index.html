Gluten Free & No Gluten Added

Know What the Label Means.

We use two different designations to help you understand what you’re buying. Gluten Free applies to pre-packaged products labelled gluten-free by the manufacturer. No Gluten Added applies to products we prepare without intentionally added gluten-containing ingredients. Davy Jones is not a gluten-free facility, and cross-contact is possible. If you have celiac disease, a severe gluten sensitivity or a food allergy, please review the product label and speak with our team before purchasing.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

What's the difference between Gluten Free and No Gluten Added?

Gluten Free products on this page are prepackaged and carry the manufacturer's gluten free designation. No Gluten Added products are made in our shop without gluten-containing ingredients. Because our shop also handles products that contain gluten, we don't describe those items as Gluten Free.

Is No Gluten Added safe for celiac disease?

We can't make that promise. If you have celiac disease or a serious sensitivity, talk to a butcher before buying. We'll explain how the item is made so you can make an informed decision.

Can I ask how a product is made?

Absolutely. Tell us which item you're considering and we'll walk you through what goes into it and how it's prepared.

Can you make something to order without gluten?

Talk to a butcher about what you're looking for. We'll go through the ingredients and preparation with you before you order.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll have your order ready. For larger orders, give us a few days' notice.

What are your hours?

Sarnia is open Monday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM, Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM, and Sunday from 10 AM – 3 PM.

Point Edward is open Tuesday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM and Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.

More questions? Our full FAQ covers ordering ahead, custom cutting, freezer packs and gluten labelling.
