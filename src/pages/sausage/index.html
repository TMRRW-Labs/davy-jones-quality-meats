FAQ

Questions we get asked

Are these really made in the shop?

Yes. The sausages on this page are ground, seasoned and stuffed in our shop.

Are they fresh or smoked?

The sausages on this page are fresh, so they need to be cooked through. Ask at the counter about any smoked products currently available.

Can you make a batch to my recipe?

Talk to a butcher about what you're looking for. Depending on the seasoning and quantity, we may be able to make a batch for you.

How are the sausages sold?

They're sold by the link.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll have your order ready. For larger orders, give us a few days so we have enough time to prepare everything.

What are your hours?

Sarnia is open Monday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM, Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM, and Sunday from 10 AM – 3 PM.

Point Edward is open Tuesday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM and Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.
