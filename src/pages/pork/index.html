FAQ

Questions we get asked

How thick should I get my pork chops?

An inch and a half to two inches gives you more room to get colour on the outside without drying out the middle. Tell us how you're planning to cook them and we'll cut them accordingly.

What's the difference between bacon and peameal bacon?

Bacon is smoked and cured pork belly. Peameal bacon is cured pork loin rolled in cornmeal. We carry both, with peameal available sliced or by the chub.

Can you cut it a specific way?

Yes. Tell us the thickness, trim or portion size you want and we'll prepare it at the counter.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll have your order ready. For larger orders, give us a few days so we have enough time to prepare everything.
