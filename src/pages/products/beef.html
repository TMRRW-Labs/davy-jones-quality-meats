FAQ

Good to Know Before You Visit.

What does “aged 21 days” mean?

Our beef is aged for a minimum of 21 days before we cut it. That extra time allows the beef to become more tender and develop a deeper, richer flavour before it reaches the case.

Why grain-fed?

We choose grain-fed beef for the marbling it develops. That marbling contributes to the texture and flavour of the beef, which is why it's an intentional part of what we buy.

Where does the ground beef come from?

From the same beef we sell in the case. It's ground in-store and our burgers are pressed here, so the ground products get the same attention as the steaks and roasts.

Can you cut it a specific way?

Absolutely. Tell us the thickness, trim, portion size or whether you want the bone left in or taken out. Custom cutting is included at no extra charge, so you can buy the cut the way you actually want to cook it.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll have it ready. For larger orders like a freezer pack, prime rib or party order, give us a few days so we have time to prepare it properly.

What are your hours?

Sarnia is open Monday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM, Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM, and Sunday from 10 AM – 3 PM.

Point Edward is open Tuesday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM and Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.

More questions? Our full FAQ covers ordering ahead, custom cutting, freezer packs and gluten labelling.
