FAQ

Questions we get asked

What is the Signature Smoky Sweet BBQ Rub?

It's our house BBQ rub. Ask about adding it to your chicken, or look for it on some of our Butcher Crafted products.

Can you flatten a whole chicken?

Yes. We'll flatten a whole fryer for the grill. You can also ask for it with our Signature Smoky Sweet BBQ Rub.

Where does the chicken come from?

Our chicken is Grade A and sourced from Ontario farms.

Can you cut it a specific way?

Yes. Tell us what you're making or how you'd like your chicken prepared and we'll help you at the counter.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll have your order ready. For larger orders, give us a few days so we have enough time to prepare everything.

What are your hours?

Sarnia is open Monday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM, Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM, and Sunday from 10 AM – 3 PM.

Point Edward is open Tuesday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM and Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.

More questions? Our full FAQ covers ordering ahead, custom cutting, freezer packs and gluten labelling.
