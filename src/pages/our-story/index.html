Our Story

Serving Sarnia-Lambton Since 1994.

For more than 30 years, Davy Jones Quality Meats has built its reputation by doing the same things well, every day. Quality meat, experienced butchers, careful preparation and dependable service have shaped what customers expect from us and what we expect from ourselves. That consistency is the standard behind everything we do.

Who We Are

Real butchers, real service.

Davy Jones Quality Meats has been serving Sarnia-Lambton since 1994 with a straightforward approach to butcher service: know the product, prepare it properly and take the time to help the customer.

That means experienced butchers behind the counter, honest answers, custom cutting and a standard of service built on knowledge, consistency and doing the work properly.
