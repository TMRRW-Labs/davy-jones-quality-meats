FAQ

Questions we get asked

How long does a freezer pack take?

Allow up to one week depending on the size of the pack and availability.

Can I change what's in a pack?

Yes. Freezer packs can be customized. Call or come in and speak with a butcher about what you'd like changed.

Are the prices fixed?

No. Freezer pack prices are subject to change with market pricing. We'll confirm the price when you order.

Can the pack be prepared to my specifications?

Yes. That's part of the freezer pack service. Tell us what you need and speak with a butcher about how you'd like your pack prepared.
