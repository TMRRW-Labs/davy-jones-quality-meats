FAQ

Questions we get asked

Can these go in an air fryer?

Yes. The appetizers and fries on this page can be cooked in an air fryer.

Are they sold frozen?

Yes. They're sold frozen by the bag.

Do you make any of these in the shop?

The fries and breaded appetizers on this page are restaurant lines we bring in. For stuffed mushrooms, jalapeño poppers and other products we prepare in-house, take a look at our Butcher Crafted collection.

Can you put together a tray for a party?

Yes. Give us a few days' notice and let us know what you're planning. Call the shop and we'll help put it together.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll have your order ready. For larger orders or party trays, give us a few days' notice.
