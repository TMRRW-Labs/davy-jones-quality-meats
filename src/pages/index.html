About Davy Jones

Local Butchers. Premium Standards.

Davy Jones Quality Meats has been a trusted part of the Sarnia-Lambton community since 1994. With shops in Sarnia and Point Edward, we’ve built our reputation on quality meat, experienced butchers and a higher standard of service.

Our Alberta beef is aged a minimum of 21 days, our burgers and sausages are made in-house, and custom cutting is always part of the service. Whether you know exactly what you want or just know what’s for dinner, our butchers are here to help.
