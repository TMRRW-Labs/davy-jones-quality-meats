FAQ

Questions we get asked

What does Butcher Crafted mean?

Butcher Crafted is the part of the case we prepare ourselves. Products are seasoned, stuffed, wrapped, ground or pressed in our shop so you can take home something that's already prepared and ready to cook.

How do I cook them?

Most Butcher Crafted products are ready for the oven or grill. Ask at the counter when you pick yours up and we'll tell you how to cook the product you're taking home.

Which of these are No Gluten Added?

Products marked No Gluten Added on this page include the pure beef burgers, Spinach & Feta Chicken Burger, and meatloaf varieties. See our Gluten Free & No Gluten Added page for more information.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call the shop and we'll have your order ready. For larger orders or party trays, give us a few days to prepare everything.

What are your hours?

Sarnia is open Monday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM, Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM, and Sunday from 10 AM – 3 PM.

Point Edward is open Tuesday to Friday from 10 AM – 6 PM and Saturday from 9 AM – 5 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.
