Privacy Policy

Last updated {{BUILD_DATE}}

Davy Jones Quality Meats is a family-run butcher shop in Sarnia, Ontario. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

Information you give us. Joining the loyalty program happens on Marsello, the loyalty platform we use to run it. The name, email address, phone number and birthday you enter there become your member record, which we can see and use. If you call the shop or speak to us at the counter, we use whatever details you give us at that point to deal with your request.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours records basic technical information: the pages visited, the referring link, the browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from the network address. We also use advertising and analytics tools that set cookies to measure whether our ads work.

Why we collect it

To identify your loyalty account at the counter, to apply your discounts, to send the member offers and birthday discount you signed up for, to answer the questions you bring to us, and to understand which of our ads and pages bring people into the shop.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run the shop and this website, including our point-of-sale and loyalty systems, our email tools, our website host, and advertising platforms. They may process your information on our behalf. We do not sell your personal information.

Email you can stop

Every marketing email we send has an unsubscribe link, and using it takes you off the list. You will still be a loyalty member and your discounts still work. Unsubscribing only stops the emails.

Cookies and advertising

We advertise on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and use their measurement tools, which set cookies in your browser. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings, and you can adjust ad personalisation in your Meta account settings. Blocking them does not stop you using this site.

How long we keep it

Loyalty records are kept for as long as you are a member. Notes we take about an order or a question are kept while we deal with them and for a reasonable period afterwards. Ask us to delete your information and we will, unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call the shop on (519) 383-8837 or ask at the counter, and tell us what you want done. Our contact page has the numbers and hours for both shops.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of the page.
